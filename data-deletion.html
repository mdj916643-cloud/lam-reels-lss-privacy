Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with LAM REELS LSS.

1. Request deletion

To request deletion, email mdj916643@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request — LAM REELS LSS.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your account, if applicable. If you cannot do that, include enough information for us to identify the relevant request or account. Do not send your password, login codes, access tokens, or other secret credentials.

2. Disconnect your Instagram account

You can stop future access by disconnecting the app or revoking its permissions in your Meta or Instagram account settings. LAM REELS LSS will no longer be able to access Instagram data through permissions that have been revoked. Revoking access does not by itself erase information already stored on your device or any records that may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

3. Data stored on your device

Drafts and settings may be stored locally on the device or browser where you use the app. Where applicable, you can remove locally stored app or site data through your browser or device settings. The exact steps depend on your browser, device, and app version.

4. What happens after a request

We will review your request and may contact you if we need clarification to identify the data. We will handle and delete eligible data in accordance with applicable requirements and the LAM REELS LSS Privacy Policy.

Contact

For data deletion questions, contact mdj916643@gmail.com.
