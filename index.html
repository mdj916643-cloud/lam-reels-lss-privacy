Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LAM REELS LSS (“the app”) handles information when you use its Instagram content-planning and account-insights features.

Important: LAM REELS LSS is intended to help you manage your own Instagram content. It does not request your Instagram password. Sign-in and any Instagram account connection must use Meta’s official authorization process.

1. Information the app may access

Depending on the features you choose and the permissions you authorize, the app may access basic Instagram account information, media and content-related information, and account insights made available through the official Instagram APIs. The app only requests information needed for the features you use.

2. How information is used

Information accessed through the Instagram APIs is used to display account information and insights, help prepare and organize content, and support publishing actions that are permitted by Meta. The app does not use this information for advertising, sell it, or use it to send direct messages.

3. Storage and retention

The app is designed to keep locally created drafts, preferences, and working data on your device. Information retrieved from Meta may be processed by the app to provide the feature you requested. Meta processes authorization and API requests under its own terms and privacy policies. Do not assume that information sent to Meta as part of an API request remains only on your device.

Local information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app, subject to your device’s own backup and storage settings. The app operator does not intend to maintain a separate external database of your personal Instagram data.

4. Sharing

The app operator does not sell or rent personal information. Information is sent to Meta only as needed for official authorization and Instagram API features you choose to use. Meta’s handling of information is governed by Meta’s own policies.

5. Security

Use the app only on a device you control, keep your device and browser/software updated, and protect access to your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Disconnecting and deleting data

You can stop using the app and disconnect its access through the relevant Meta/Instagram account settings. You can delete locally stored app data using the app’s available deletion controls or your device settings. If you need help with a privacy request, contact us using the email below.

7. Third-party services

Instagram and Meta are third-party services. Your use of those services is also subject to their terms and privacy policies. LAM REELS LSS is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app’s features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.
